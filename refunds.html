30-day refund policy

MLDY Labs, Alberta, Canada, offers a full refund when you request it within 30 days of a charge for Arcana Forge. This applies to initial subscription payments and renewal charges.

Email arcanaforgeapp@gmail.com from your account email, with the payment date or receipt number. Do not send card details. Support will confirm your request and any receipt information needed. Approved refunds go to the original payment method; your bank determines when they appear.

A refund ends the paid access associated with that charge and cancels future renewal. Your local journals and artwork remain on your device. Canceling a subscription without requesting a refund normally leaves access until the end of the paid period.

The seven-day trial requires no payment card and creates no charge. Paid subscriptions begin only when you separately choose a paid plan and complete checkout.

This policy adds to your rights under applicable consumer law and does not limit those rights. Payments are not enabled in the current preview.

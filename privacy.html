Privacy notice

MLDY Labs, Alberta, Canada, is responsible for Arcana Forge's handling of personal information. For access, correction, deletion, or privacy questions, email arcanaforgeapp@gmail.com. Updated October 6, 2026.

What is stored

Your account service stores your email address, a password hash, session identifiers, and trial and subscription records needed to verify access. It does not store your password in plain text. Stripe handles checkout and payment information; Arcana Forge does not store full payment card details.

Reading journals, saved profiles, birth details entered into profiles, and imported artwork are stored locally on your device. These local files are not uploaded by the account and billing service. Information you choose to send to support, such as screenshots or logs, is received by MLDY Labs.

Why it is used

Account data is used to sign you in, administer your seven-day trial, verify paid access, prevent repeated trial claims, and respond to support or refund requests. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers and connections

Render hosts the account service and database. Stripe processes payments and subscription events. GitHub distributes releases and update downloads. Vercel hosts this website. These providers may process connection logs and information outside Canada, including in the United States, under their own privacy policies. The application contacts the account service and GitHub when account or update features run.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, we count website visitors, page views, visible active pages, and download or important button clicks. A random identifier is stored only for the current analytics day and is separate for each website. We do not collect names, email addresses, full URLs, query strings, referrers, location, or browser fingerprints for these counters. Identifiers are hashed on the server. Raw analytics records are deleted after 48 hours. Your analytics choice is remembered for up to 180 days. Use Analytics preferences in the footer to change your choice; withdrawing consent stops collection and deletes today’s records for this browser. We honor Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. Declining analytics does not affect website features.

Vercel hosts the tracking endpoint. Render stores the limited analytics records in a restricted database schema. Hosting providers may still process network access logs to operate and secure their services. Authorized MLDY Labs desktop monitors display summary counts, page names, and click activity without personal details. Analytics days use America/Denver time.

Retention and your choices

Local information remains under your control until you delete it. Uninstalling the application preserves your local journals and artwork. Account and transaction records are kept as needed for account access, trial eligibility, support, refunds, and legal recordkeeping. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion; we will explain any records that must be retained and why. Removing an account may end access. Backup copies may remain until their retention cycle completes.

We use password hashing, encrypted network connections, and restricted access to protect account information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Do not include sensitive personal details in a support message unless necessary to resolve your issue.

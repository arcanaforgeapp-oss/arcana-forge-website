Understanding Arcana Forge numerology

Numerology assigns symbolic themes to numbers calculated from a name or birth date. Arcana Forge uses these calculations for personal exploration. They do not measure ability, establish personality as a fact or determine what will happen.

Life Path and Birthday Number

The Life Path calculation adds the digits of the birth date, then reduces the total. Birthday Number reduces the day of the month. The app preserves 11, 22 and 33 when they occur during reduction. Different numerology traditions use different methods, so results can differ between tools.

Worked birth-date example

For July 14, 1990, add 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 + 0 + 7 + 1 + 4 = 31, then 3 + 1 = 4. The Life Path is 4. The day 14 reduces to 1 + 4 = 5 for the Birthday Number. Use these numbers as prompts: where could structure help, and where could flexibility help?

Name calculations

The app maps A through I to 1 through 9 and repeats that pattern through Z. Expression uses all letters, Soul Urge uses A, E, I, O and U, and Personality uses the remaining letters. Punctuation and spaces are ignored. This implementation uses the English alphabet and treats Y as a consonant.

A short name example

For “Ada,” A = 1, D = 4 and A = 1. Expression totals 6. The vowels total 2 for Soul Urge, and the consonant totals 4 for Personality. This example shows the arithmetic; it is not a claim that a name determines a person.

Personal Year, Month and Day

Personal Year adds your birth month, birth day and the digits of the current year before reduction. Personal Month adds the current calendar month to the Personal Year; Personal Day adds the current day to the Personal Month. These are calendar-based reflection prompts, not deadlines or forecasts.

Use the result constructively

Choose one theme that fits a situation and one that does not. Ask what evidence from your actual experience supports your interpretation. You do not need to enter your full legal name to experiment.
